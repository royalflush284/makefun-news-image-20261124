THE CAPITAL WIRE is a one-page broadsheet that compresses the whole of
Tuesday 24 November 2026 into a single 1024x1024 news image: 37 stories
across 9 labelled regions, every one of them dated to the day.

The front page leads with hard news. Ukraine sinks the Russian warship
Merkury off Libya with seven sea drones launched at Benghazi; Kyiv
strikes kill at least 15 near the capital, in a block of flats in
Dymer; and Israeli strikes kill 3 in Gaza, a UN worker among the dead.
A stat strip across the top carries the day in numbers: gold at
$3,588 an ounce, Bitcoin near $61,284, 15 killed near Kyiv, no deal at
COP31, the House at 258-173, Liverpool 4-0 over Manchester United and
$3.8bn for GTA VI on day one.

Thirteen scene cards tell the biggest stories in full, a colour region
bar counts the day region by region (Europe 7, Middle East 4, Africa
1, Americas 10, Asia 1, South Asia 5, Asia-Pacific 3, Oceania 2, World
4 — 37 in total), a dotted world map pins each numbered story where it
happened, and an ALSO ON THE WIRE index carries stories 14-37 in one
readable line each. A timeline shows the times as reported, and a date
check strip names the earlier-day stories deliberately left out.

Sources are named on every story, reported facts are kept apart from
claims, and the picture and map are original, drawn in code. Nothing
on the page reaches past what its named source reported: tolls stay
attributed, a strike claim stays a claim, and the day's numbers are
printed exactly as they were reported on the day.

Run it with: python3 main.py > entry.png
